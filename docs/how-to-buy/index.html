Buying guide

How to buy

Jalu Press accepts two stablecoins only: USDC and USDT. USDC can be sent on Solana or Polygon, and USDT on TRC20 (Tron) or Polygon. Prices are in US dollars and you pay the same number of tokens, plus a few unique hundredths of a cent that identify your order (for example, $12 = 12.0347 USDC).

Step by step

  1. Pick your product and open its checkout.
  2. Enter your email so we know where to send your files. A name is optional.
  3. Choose a network: USDC on Solana or Polygon, or USDT on TRC20 or Polygon.
  4. Email us your order with the button on the checkout page. This step is required: it tells us which payment is yours.
  5. Send the exact amount shown (for example 12.0347 USDC), to the address shown, on the network shown. The last digits are unique to your order. Network fees are paid by the sender and are on top of the price.
  6. Receive your files. Our system checks the blockchain every few minutes. Once your payment is final, your files are emailed to you automatically as an attachment, usually within 30 minutes.
Check the network before you send. Send only the token and network shown at checkout: USDC on Solana or Polygon, or USDT on Tron (TRC20) or Polygon. Tokens sent on another network (such as Ethereum or BNB Chain) or a different coin cannot be recovered. On Polygon, send native USDC, not the older bridged USDC.e. If you pay from an exchange, choose the matching network in its withdrawal screen.
