Just engaged? Start here, calmly

The first weeks after a proposal are full of questions: how much can we spend, how many people can we invite, and what needs booking first? This planner gives those questions an order, so you can enjoy being engaged while the plan quietly takes shape.

You begin with the big picture and your priorities, written separately and then compared, so the money and energy go where you both care most. Then come the budget, the guest list, the suppliers and a planning timeline that runs from the first conversations to the thank-you notes.

Every page is fillable on screen and prints cleanly in Letter or A4. Print the vendor, seating and run-sheet pages to share with family, a planner or the friend who will hold the phone on the day.

A spreadsheet that keeps the whole plan up to date

Type your wedding date, currency symbol, budget, guest cap and meal options once on the Setup tab. The Budget tab works out balances, the next payment and its date for every line. Guests & RSVP counts yes, no and pending replies, meals and travel needs as you type. The Vendors tab keeps quotes, contracts and payment dates together.

The Timeline tab comes with a starter task list whose due dates are counted back from your wedding date, with overdue tasks flagged. The Dashboard puts days to go, budget used and left, payments due in the next 30 days, the RSVP summary and the tasks that need attention on one screen.

Made for any wedding, anywhere. Any currency, any guest count, and an optional page for destination weddings and families in two countries. It gives no legal, contract or tax advice; there is simply a space to note what your local registrar tells you.
