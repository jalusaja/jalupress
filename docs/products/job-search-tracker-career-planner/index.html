See every application, follow up on time, walk in prepared

Applications in one tab, interview notes in another, names of contacts in your head. A job search is a lot to keep track of, and the follow-ups are the first thing to slip. This planner puts the whole search in one place: what you are aiming for, where you have applied, what needs a nudge, how to prepare for each conversation and how to compare what you are offered.

The book starts with a goals page for your target roles, work setup and weekly numbers, then a search plan with a space for a short introduction. A weekly planner and application logs keep the week and every application on paper, and a pipeline board shows the whole search at a glance. Company research sheets ask you to match what a role asks for with something you have actually done.

For interviews there is a prep sheet, STAR story builders and a bank of sixteen original story prompts to get you started on your own true stories, plus an interview log and a post-interview reflection page. A skills and achievements inventory helps you find the stories. A networking log and conversation planner keep track of who you spoke to and what you promised, an offer comparison sheet lines up what each offer actually says, and a follow-up checklist ties it together. Everything is fillable on screen and prints cleanly in Letter or A4.

A spreadsheet that keeps count

On Setup, enter the date your search starts, a weekly application goal, a weekly contacts goal and how many days ahead you want a follow-up warning, then list your target roles and the places you look.

Applications has a drop-down status (Saved, Applied, Screening, Interview, Offer, Rejected, Withdrawn), a date applied and a next follow-up date. A follow-up in the past shows OVERDUE and one inside your warning window shows DUE SOON. Interviews, with a SEND THANK-YOU reminder for a week after each one, and Networking, with its own reach-out dates, work the same way.

The Weekly Summary counts each week of your search: applications sent against your goal, replies, response rate (replies divided by applications sent), interviews held and people contacted. The Dashboard shows counts by status as a funnel chart, this week against your goal, the next follow-ups due and a twelve-week chart.

Pay fields are free text, blank for you to fill in as listed. Rows marked “Example” are made up to show how it works; type over them or clear them.

This planner is an organising tool, not legal, immigration, tax or salary advice. Rules differ by country; check local sources or ask a qualified professional. It makes no promise about interviews, offers or hiring results, contains no salary figures, and does not connect to or sync with any job board, email or calendar.
