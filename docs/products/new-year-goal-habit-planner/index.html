A few goals, with a real plan behind each one

A fresh year makes it tempting to write a long list of resolutions and hope for the best. This planner takes a calmer route: look back first, choose a small number of goals that genuinely matter to you, and give each one a plan you can start this week.

It opens with a year review and a check-in on eight areas of life, so you can see where a small change would help most. Then you pick a word for the year, list up to eight goals, and turn the important ones into goal action plans: what done looks like, why it matters, milestones with dates, the first three steps, and what you will do if something gets in the way.

A year-by-quarter page spreads the milestones across four seasons, and monthly and weekly pages keep the next step in front of you without turning the year into one giant to-do list.

Habits you can actually keep, and a spreadsheet that keeps count

Goals move forward through small, repeated actions. The habit builder helps you design a habit before you start it, with a cue, a tiny first version and a way to track it. Then tick boxes on the monthly tracker for up to twelve habits, fill a whole year of one habit on the year-in-habits grid, or take on a 100-day challenge.

Weekly, monthly and quarterly reviews and a mid-year reset help you adjust without starting over. The companion spreadsheet works in Microsoft Excel and Google Sheets: list goals and milestones, tick daily habits, and goal progress, habit totals by month and a one-screen dashboard update by themselves.

Undated, so it starts when you do. Begin in January, on a birthday or on any Monday. The planner never tells you what your goals should be; it simply helps you follow through on the ones you choose.
