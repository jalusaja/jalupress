One place for the whole season

The holidays come with a lot of small lists: who you are buying for, what you have already bought, which cards still need stamps, who is bringing dessert. This planner gathers them into one binder, so you can see where things stand at a glance instead of keeping it all in your head.

Start with the big picture: key dates, traditions you want to keep, and the total you have decided to spend, split into categories like gifts, food, decorations and cards. Then add everyone you buy for to the gift list, with a planned amount, the gift, what it actually cost, and ticks for bought, wrapped and given.

Log purchases once in the spending tracker, keep parcels and return dates on the orders page, and work through the holiday card list. For gatherings there is an event planner, a guest list with RSVPs, a holiday meal plan, a cooking timeline and a grocery list by store section.

A countdown, a wrap-up, and a spreadsheet that adds it up

A gentle week-by-week countdown runs from six weeks out to the day itself, with space for your own tasks. Afterwards, a thank-you tracker and a post-holiday wrap-up capture what worked, where decorations are stored and gift ideas for next year.

The companion spreadsheet works in Microsoft Excel and Google Sheets. Enter the amounts you choose, list the people on your gift list, and log each purchase with a category. Totals per category and per person, what is left of your own budget, and a one-screen dashboard update by themselves.

Your numbers, your traditions. Works for any winter holiday and any currency. The planner never suggests what to spend; it simply keeps the plan you made in view.
