Know your costs, ship on time, see where the money goes

Orders in one place, stock in another, prices in your head, and receipts in a drawer. This planner brings a one-person product business together: what you make, what it costs, what you charge, what is waiting to ship and what is left at the end of the month.

The book starts with a business snapshot and a year at a glance, then gives you twelve monthly planner pages with a calendar, a list of orders to ship, things to make or restock, posts and market dates, and a money check. Order log sheets and made-to-order cards keep each order and its due date visible. Inventory and materials sheets track what is on hand against a reorder level, and product costing worksheets turn materials, your time, packaging and any fee % you type into a unit cost and a suggested price.

Supplier contacts, a product launch checklist and a one-page monthly review finish the book. Everything is fillable on screen and prints cleanly in Letter or A4.

A spreadsheet that does the adding up

On Setup, enter your business name, currency symbol, the month your business year starts, an hourly rate for your own time, a default target margin, and your own sales channels and cost categories. Everything else reads from there.

Product Pricing works out unit cost (materials, labour hours × rate, packaging and other costs), a suggested price for your target margin, and the margin at the price you choose, and flags products that fall below target. Orders tracks each order with quantity, price, shipping, the fees you paid, a status and a ship-by date; open orders past their date show OVERDUE and those coming up show DUE SOON. Inventory & Materials shows LOW STOCK when on hand reaches the reorder level.

Income & Expenses holds your other income and every business cost. The Monthly Profit Summary adds up twelve months of order sales, other income, fees, expenses and profit, with a breakdown by cost category and charts, and the Dashboard pulls it into one view for the month you choose. A Launch & Content Calendar adds a countdown checklist and a posting calendar.

Fee % and fixed fee fields are blank on purpose, because fees differ between shops and change over time: you type the figures you actually pay. Rows marked “Example” are made up to show how it works; type over them or clear them.

This planner is an organising tool, not tax, legal or accounting advice. Check your local rules or ask a qualified professional. It does not recommend prices, fees or tax treatment, contains no tax rates or platform fee figures, and does not connect to or sync with any shop, payment or accounting service.
