A day you can actually keep up with

Night feeds, a sleep stretch that runs past midnight, a bottle, a diaper, and the wish that yesterday’s notes were still in one place. This tracker keeps the first months in one book: what happened, when, and a total you can glance at before a visit or a handover.

The core page is a 24-hour daily log, designed to be printed many times. One sheet holds breast minutes on the left and right, a bottle amount and type, sleep start and end, and wet or dirty marks, plus a box for the day’s totals. A weekly overview, a pumping log, and a caregiver handover sheet sit alongside it.

Growth, appointments, visits and vaccinations, contacts and firsts are running lists you fill in from your own appointments and memories. The vaccination page has blank fields only — it does not include a schedule. Monthly photo-and-notes pages, and a year at a glance, are there as a light first-year keepsake, written in your own words. Every page is fillable on screen and prints cleanly in Letter or A4.

A spreadsheet that adds up the day

On Setup, enter a name, a birth date, and the units you want to use: millilitres or fluid ounces, kilograms or pounds, and centimetres or inches. Type later figures in those units. If you switch units later, existing numbers are not converted for you.

The Daily Log is a time-stamped list of feeds, sleep and diapers. If a sleep stretch continues past midnight, enter the start and end times on the same row, on the date the sleep began; the duration is calculated across midnight automatically. The Pumping Log totals left and right amounts. Daily and Weekly Summary adds up feeds, breast minutes, bottle amounts, sleep, diapers and pumping, and shows simple charts for the last 14 days. The Dashboard shows today’s totals, the time of the last feed, and a few records you have entered yourself.

Rows marked “(example)” show how it works; they are made up. Type over them or clear them.

This tracker is a record-keeping tool, not medical advice. Ask your doctor, midwife or health visitor about your baby's health. It does not recommend feeding amounts, sleep schedules, “normal” ranges, growth percentiles, vaccine timetables, medicine doses or sleep arrangements, and it does not connect to any baby app.
