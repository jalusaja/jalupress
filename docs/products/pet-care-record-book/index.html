Everything about your pet, in one place

Vaccine certificates in a drawer, the vet's number in one phone, feeding instructions on a sticky note, a receipt for flea treatment somewhere in an inbox. This record book gathers it all: who your pet is, who looks after them, what they eat, what was given and when, and what comes next.

There are pages for everyday care (a weekly checklist, a feeding plan, grooming, walks and training) and for health records (weight, vaccinations, preventives, a medication dose tracker, a vet-visit log and a before-and-after page for each appointment).

When you travel, two pet-sitter pages and a travel and boarding checklist are ready to fill in and leave on the counter. An emergency and lost-pet sheet keeps a photo, markings, microchip number and first steps on one page. Every page is fillable on screen and prints cleanly in Letter or A4.

A spreadsheet that knows what is due

List up to eight pets. On the Care Schedule tab, add each repeating task (a check-up, a monthly preventive, a nail trim, a license renewal) with the date it was last done and how many days until the next one, as your vet or routine says. The next due date, days left and an OK, Due soon or Overdue status fill in for you.

The Health Log, Weight Log and Expenses tabs keep the rest. The Dashboard shows each pet's age, latest weight, next task and spending this year, plus spending by month and category.

Rows marked “Example” show how it works; type over them or clear them.

A record book, not veterinary advice. It does not recommend any vaccine, medicine, food or schedule; the intervals you enter come from your vet or your own routine. Follow your veterinarian’s instructions, and contact your vet or an emergency clinic if your pet seems unwell.

Inside the record book

  • About your pet: a pet profile with a photo box, and important contacts (vet, emergency clinic, groomer, sitter, insurance, microchip registry)
  • Planning: a year at a glance for vaccines, check-ups, preventives and renewals
  • Everyday care: a weekly daily-care checklist, a feeding plan and food log, grooming, walks and activity, and training goals
  • Health records: weight, vaccinations, preventives, a medication dose tracker, a vet-visit log, a before-and-after visit page and observation notes
  • When you are away: two pet-sitter instruction pages and a travel and boarding checklist
  • Just in case, costs and memories: an emergency and lost-pet sheet, an expenses tracker, a year in review, milestones and notes
