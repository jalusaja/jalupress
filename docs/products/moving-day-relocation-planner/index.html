The whole move, in one folder

Quotes in one inbox, a half-finished list on the fridge, boxes labelled “misc”, and a nagging feeling that someone still has the old address. This planner pulls a move together: when it happens, who is helping, what it costs, what is in every box, and who needs to know where you have gone.

Start with a move overview, then work through a week-by-week timeline from eight weeks out to moving week. Compare mover and van quotes with the questions worth asking, keep a moving budget and an expense log, and track address changes and utility stop and start dates — with room for the last four digits of a reference, never a full account number.

For packing there is a supplies list, a declutter plan, a sell-and-donate tracker, two pages of numbered box inventory and an open-first essentials box. Moving day gets an hour-by-hour plan, a kids-and-pets page and move-out and move-in checks. In the new home, a floor-plan grid, an unpacking plan, a contacts list and a getting-to-know-the-area page help you settle. Every page is fillable on screen and prints cleanly in Letter or A4.

A spreadsheet that keeps the move on track

On Setup, enter the moving date, a total budget and the towns you are moving from and to. The Timeline tab holds a starter list of tasks, each set a number of weeks before moving day; due dates are worked out for you, and overdue tasks or tasks due in the next seven days are highlighted.

The Quotes tab compares base price, extras and deposit and highlights the lowest total. The Budget tab compares what you planned with actuals from the Expense Log. The Box Inventory tracks each box from packed to arrived to unpacked, and flags a box number used twice. The Address Changes tab lists the kinds of organisations that usually need your new address.

The Dashboard shows days to go, tasks done, overdue tasks, budget used, box progress, address changes completed and boxes by room.

Rows marked “Example” show how it works; type over them or clear them.
