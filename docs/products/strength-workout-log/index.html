A log for the next twelve weeks of training

A new training block is easier to keep when the plan and the record live in the same place. This log gives you a 12-week calendar, a weekly split planner, and session sheets you can reprint — plus a spreadsheet that adds up volume, personal bests and cardio minutes as you go.

It is made for gym sessions, home training or a mix of both, in kilograms or pounds. Write in the PDF, type on the page, or keep the running totals in the spreadsheet. Use one, the other, or both.

Every page is fillable on screen and prints cleanly in Letter or A4. Reprint the session sheets, the cardio log and the notes pages as often as you need.

A spreadsheet that keeps the totals

Choose kilograms or pounds, how many days you train each week, a start date and a split — full-body, upper-lower, push-pull-legs, or your own. The Exercise Library lists forty common movements you can edit. The Program Builder holds twelve weeks of up to six sessions.

Log each exercise with sets, reps, load and an optional RPE. Volume (sets × reps × load) and an estimated 1-rep max fill in automatically. The estimate uses the Epley formula, load × (1 + reps/30), and is labelled “estimated (Epley)”. A “PB” flag appears when that row is your best estimated 1-rep max for the exercise in this file. The Dashboard then shows weekly volume, a personal-best table, sessions per week, and strength days next to cardio minutes.

Rows marked “Example” show how it works; type over them or clear them.

Yours to keep, with no app tie-in. The journal and the spreadsheet do not connect to any training or health app, and they do not import or sync a history for you. Consult a professional before starting a new exercise program.
