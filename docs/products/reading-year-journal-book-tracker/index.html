A quiet place for the year of books ahead

Some years of reading disappear into a stack of finished titles and a vague sense that you read “quite a lot”. This journal gives those books a home: a log you can keep as you go, review pages for the ones that stay with you, and a look back at the end of the month and the year.

It is made for adult readers, book clubs and the person buying a gift for someone who always has a book on the go. Write in the PDF, type on the page, or keep the running totals in the spreadsheet. Use one, the other, or both.

Every page is fillable on screen and prints cleanly in Letter or A4. Reprint the log, the reviews, the TBR shelves and the wrap-ups as often as you need.

A spreadsheet that adds the year up as you go

Type the reading year and an optional book, page or hours goal once on the Setup tab. Log each book with its format, dates, genre and rating. Days to finish fill in when both dates are there. The Dashboard then shows goal progress, books and pages by month, a genre and format split, your average rating and a top five.

There are tabs for a TBR list, a series tracker and book-club meetings. Rows marked “Example” show how it works; type over them or clear them. A book counts toward the goal when it has a finish date in the reading year and is not marked DNF.

Print, ebook or audio — and no app tie-in. The journal and the spreadsheet are yours to keep. They do not connect to any bookstore, library app or reading site, and they do not import a library for you.
