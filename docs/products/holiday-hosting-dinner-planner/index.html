The whole gathering, on paper before the doorbell rings

Replies scattered across texts, a menu in your head, one oven and five dishes that all need it at four o'clock, and a cousin who mentioned an allergy you cannot quite remember. This planner puts a holiday gathering in one place: who is coming, what they can eat, what you are serving, what to buy, and what needs doing when.

Start with an event overview and a guest list with RSVPs and party sizes. A dietary needs tracker keeps what each guest has told you next to the dishes that suit them. Plan the menu course by course, decide who is bringing what, copy recipes onto fillable recipe cards, and turn it all into a grocery list sorted by store aisle.

A prep countdown runs from two weeks out to the night before, then an hour-by-hour plan and an oven and stovetop schedule carry you through the day. There is a seating plan with a name box for every seat, a place-setting check, a house-prep checklist, overnight-guest pages with a house-info card, a drinks planner with alcohol-free options, and a helpers-and-jobs list. Afterwards, log leftovers, track thank-you notes and write down what to keep for next year. A season-at-a-glance page covers Thanksgiving through New Year's Day. Every page is fillable on screen and prints cleanly in Letter or A4.

A spreadsheet that keeps the gathering on track

On Setup, enter the date, the time the meal is served and when guests arrive. The Guests tab tracks invitations, RSVPs, party sizes, kids, dietary needs, what each guest is bringing and who is staying over; confirmed headcount is added up for you.

The Countdown tab holds a starter list of hosting jobs, each set a number of days before the event; due dates are worked out and overdue or due-soon jobs are highlighted. The Cooking Schedule works out when each dish should start from its cooking and resting minutes and the time you want it ready. The Grocery List sorts items by aisle and totals what is still to buy, and the Leftovers tab shows a use-by date from the number of days you choose.

The Dashboard shows days to go, confirmed guests, dietary needs, overnight guests, menu coverage by course, countdown progress, groceries left to buy and the first oven start time.

Rows marked “(example)” show how it works; type over them or clear them.

A planning tool, not professional advice. It does not give medical, nutrition or food-safety advice. Always confirm allergies and dietary needs directly with each guest, read ingredient labels, and follow your own recipes and official food-safety guidance for cooking times, temperatures and storing leftovers. Keep door codes and passwords out of the files.
