One system the whole family can see

Who fed the cat? Whose turn is it to clear the table? Is the school bag packed? Most families answer the same questions every day. This planner puts the jobs, the routines and the rewards on paper where everyone can see them, so children can check the chart and tick it off themselves.

The book starts with a family page, a page of chore ideas grouped by age and a who-does-what list for the whole household. Then come three weekly chore charts with points and a goal, a family chart for the fridge, and a big-box star chart for younger children who are just starting out.

Routine cards cover mornings, after school, evenings and weekends, with a tick for every step and every day. A reward menu, a 50-star tracker, a points bank log and two well-done certificates make the effort visible, and a weekly schedule, monthly calendar, clubs tracker and family meeting page keep the week organised. Everything is fillable on screen and prints cleanly in Letter or A4.

A spreadsheet that adds up the week

On Setup, type the date the week starts, what you call your points (points, stars, coins) and up to six children with a weekly goal.

On the Chore Chart, list each chore, pick the child and type what it is worth. Each day, pick a tick for done or a cross for not done. The ticks, missed days, points and a done percentage appear for you, and the day headings follow your week-starting date.

The Points Log is the points bank: add the week's total, bonus points and rewards swapped. The Rewards tab shows each child's balance and READY when they have enough, and the Dashboard shows points against each goal, the balance and charts. Rows marked “Example” are made up to show how it works; type over them or clear them.

This planner is a family organising tool with general ideas, not parenting, child-development, medical or professional advice. The chore ideas are not milestones: every child is different. Show a child how first, and keep anything hot, sharp, heavy or chemical for adults or close supervision.
