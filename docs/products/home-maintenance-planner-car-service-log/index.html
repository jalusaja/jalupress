Know what is due, and what was done

Filters, smoke alarm tests, gutters, the boiler, the car's next service: most of us remember a job only when something stops working, and then cannot find the model number, the warranty date or the name of the person who fixed it last time. This planner keeps the whole picture in one place: what to check each month and season, when each appliance and vehicle was last serviced, what repairs cost and what is due next.

The book starts with a home profile and an appliance index, then monthly and quarterly checklists with a tick box for every month or quarter, and four seasonal checklists (spring, summer, autumn and winter) that you can rename for your own climate. A yearly calendar, appliance record cards, a smoke and carbon monoxide alarm test log, a repair and improvement log, a contractor and handyperson contact list and a project planner follow.

For vehicles there is a profile page for up to three cars, service logs, and a tyre (tire) and fluid check sheet. A yearly cost summary pulls the home and vehicle spending together. Everything is fillable on screen and prints cleanly in Letter or A4.

A spreadsheet that works out what is due

On Setup, choose your home type, km or miles, your currency and how many days ahead you want a warning, then name up to three vehicles and type their current odometer readings.

Recurring Tasks has a drop-down for how often a job repeats (Monthly, Quarterly, Twice a year, Yearly or Custom days). Type the date you last did it and the next due date appears, with a status of OVERDUE, DUE SOON or On track. Appliances works the same way from the service interval you type, and Vehicle Log reminds you by date, by distance or both, using the intervals from your own owner's manual.

The Repair Log and Vehicle Log feed a Cost Summary by area, vehicle and month, and the Dashboard lists everything overdue or due in the next days you chose, with charts. Rows marked “Example” are made up to show how it works; type over them or clear them.

This planner is a reminder and record-keeping tool, not a substitute for the manufacturer's manual or a qualified electrician, plumber, gas engineer or mechanic. It gives no safety guarantees or professional advice, and the service intervals are yours: check your owner's manual.
