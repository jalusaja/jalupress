About Jalu Press

Jalu Press publishes practical English ebooks and light tools: printables, workbooks and trackers that help people organize one important part of life.

Each product is original, written and designed in-house, and tested to print well and work in the free tools most people already have. Where a product draws on public guidance, the sources are credited inside it.

How we work

  • Digital only. Every product is a download. Nothing physical is shipped.
  • Crypto checkout. We accept USDC on Solana or Polygon, and USDT on TRC20 or Polygon only.
  • Light on data. No accounts, no tracking scripts, no advertising cookies. See Privacy.
  • For readers worldwide. Products are written in plain English and avoid single-country assumptions wherever they can.
