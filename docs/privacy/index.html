Privacy

Short version: we collect as little as possible.

  • No tracking. This site uses no analytics, advertising or social tracking scripts and sets no cookies.
  • Hosting. The site is a static website hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log basic technical data such as IP addresses for security, under its own privacy statement.
  • Checkout. The details you type on the checkout page stay in your browser. For a crypto order they are only sent to us if you choose to email us your order. For a PayPal order, the product, amount and (if you entered one) delivery email are sent to PayPal to complete payment. We use this information only to deliver your files and answer you.
  • PayPal. If you pay with PayPal you are taken to PayPal's website. PayPal processes your payment under its own privacy policy. We receive a payment notice with your PayPal name, email, amount and item, and use it only to deliver your files and for order support.
  • Crypto payments. Blockchain transactions are public by nature. We see the sending address, amount and time of your payment, as anyone can.
  • Never shared or sold. We do not sell or share your details, and we do not send marketing email unless you ask for it.
  • Free sample pack. If you enter your email to get the free pack, the form is delivered to us by FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), a free form service. We keep your address, the date and the page you signed up on in a private file on our own computer, send you the pack and one short follow-up about our bundle, and nothing else. Every email has an unsubscribe link (reply with "Unsubscribe" in the subject) and we remove you on request. We never share or sell the list.
  • What we never ask for on this site: passwords, card numbers, private keys or seed phrases. If you pay by card, you enter those details on PayPal's site, not on ours.

You can ask us to delete your order emails at any time using the support address shown on the checkout page.
