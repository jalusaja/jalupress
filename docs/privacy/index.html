Privacy

Short version: we collect as little as possible.

  • No tracking. This site uses no analytics, advertising or social tracking scripts and sets no cookies.
  • Hosting. The site is a static website hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log basic technical data such as IP addresses for security, under its own privacy statement.
  • Checkout. The details you type on the checkout page stay in your browser. They are only sent to us if you choose to email us your order, and we use them only to deliver your files and answer you.
  • Payments. Blockchain transactions are public by nature. We see the sending address, amount and time of your payment, as anyone can.
  • Never shared or sold. We do not sell or share your details, and we do not send marketing email unless you ask for it.
  • What we never ask for: passwords, card numbers, private keys or seed phrases.

You can ask us to delete your order emails at any time using the support address shown on the checkout page.
