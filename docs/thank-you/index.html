Thank you for your order

If you paid with PayPal, your files will be emailed to you, usually within 15 minutes. It is not instant.

  1. Now: PayPal confirms your payment and sends us a notice, usually within a minute or two.
  2. Every 10 minutes: our system checks for new payments and emails your files as a ZIP attachment.
  3. If nothing has arrived after 30 minutes: check your spam or junk folder, then write to hallo@jalupublishing.com with your order reference.

We send the files to the email address on your PayPal account, and to the delivery email you entered at checkout if you used that option. Please check your spam or junk folder if you do not see the email.

You do not need to email us first. If the files have not arrived after 30 minutes and they are not in your spam folder, write to hallo@jalupublishing.com with your PayPal transaction details or order reference, and we will help. (If PayPal marks your payment as pending, for example an eCheck, delivery waits until it clears.)
